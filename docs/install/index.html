Step-by-Step Installation Guide (iQOO Neo 10 / Funtouch OS)

Follow these 4 simple steps to install the APK safely on Vivo/iQOO devices running Android 15.

1

Download the APK

Tap the Download Release APK button above in Chrome or Vivo Browser. If your browser asks "File might be harmful. Do you want to download RallyPoint-release.apk anyway?", tap Download anyway.

2

Allow "Install Unknown Apps"

When you tap the downloaded file, Funtouch OS may say "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source". Tap Settings and toggle on "Allow from this source".

3

Bypass Play Protect Warning

Google Play Protect may display "Unrecognized app" because the APK is installed directly from GitHub. Tap "More details" and then tap "Install anyway" to complete installation.

4

Disable Funtouch Battery Killing

Funtouch OS has aggressive background battery management. Open RallyPoint, go to Settings > tap Funtouch Battery Guide, and set Battery usage to Unrestricted so courtside scoring never gets killed!
